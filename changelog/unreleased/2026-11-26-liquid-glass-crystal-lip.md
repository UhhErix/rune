- **Liquid Glass has a crystal lip.** On `LiquidGlass::regular()` and `LiquidGlass::clear()`, the body tint (`tint`), Clear's bright-backdrop dimming and Regular's dark tone now thin out across the bevel toward the rim (up to 70% at the edge, `apple::LIP_CLARITY`), so the refracted backdrop reads through the edge of dark or heavily tinted glass instead of being painted over. The flat interior is unchanged, as are `BlurOnly`, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and the web presets. Apps with glass goldens over a non-black backdrop will see a brighter rim band.
