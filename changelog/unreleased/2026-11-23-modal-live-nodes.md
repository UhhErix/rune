- **`El::modal_live` keeps chosen nodes live while a modal is open.** Call it after `modal` or `modal_tx` with the keys of the nodes to keep: those nodes and their descendants keep hover, their cursor and presses under the open modal, while Tab, focus and assistive technology stay inside the modal. A press on a live node first dismisses the open modals that dismiss on an outside press, innermost first, then delivers the press in the same frame. With nested modals a node stays live only when each open modal names it, so a dialog over a menu makes the menu's bar inert again. Modals without it behave as before.
