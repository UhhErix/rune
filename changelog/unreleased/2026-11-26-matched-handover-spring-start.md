- Motion tooling: a matched flight that sets off from rest no longer fails `bad-handover` for flying far. The target's first step only has to start the spring (the same way as the next step and at most half of it), with no fixed 12 pt cap, so a 1,100 pt flight's 15.5 pt first step pairs cleanly. The step across a handover from a moving source is now judged by `jerk` at its usual threshold, as it would be on one track. `scripts/motion-audit/analyze.py` drops the cap the same way.
