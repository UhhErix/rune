- Motion tooling: a matched flight that sets off from rest no longer fails `bad-handover` for flying far. Its first step only has to start the spring (the same way as the next step and at most half of it), with no fixed 12 pt cap, so a 1,100 pt flight's 15.5 pt first step pairs cleanly. The step across a handover from a moving source is now judged by `jerk` at its usual threshold and on what shows, as it would be on one track. `scripts/motion-audit/analyze.py`, which has no jerk rule, keeps the 12 pt cap for an axis the source was moving on and drops it from rest. _Checked with the `motion_gate` and `test_analyze.py` tests and an app's Store and Library motion gates._
